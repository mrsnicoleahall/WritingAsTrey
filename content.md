# Chapter 02
Label: The Vow
Title: Goals

Body:
My dream is to become the best writer I possibly can, while being able to share my mind with others — hopefully making a meaningful and lasting impact on those who take the time to read my works. I would rather change one person's life than just be mentioned by thousands.

I find the working life absolutely dreadful and boring: repeating the same things, day after day, over and over. If I can become a real writer, I can escape that fate.

Quote: It is meaningless just to live, and it's meaningless to just fight. I want to win!
Quote-By: Ichigo Kurosaki — Bleach

# Chapter 03
Label: Scars
Title: Childhood and Past

Body:
As a kid, I was never perfect. I have ADHD, never truly fit in, and had a rough situation with my parents that meant living with my grandparents. Elementary school went relatively fine — I was immature, sure, but that was all.

Middle school was harder. I found myself trying to be something I wasn't. I was heavily depressed, stuck getting back together and breaking up with an ex, never really understanding what I was supposed to do. I hated labels, hated being lectured, hated being the odd one out. I overthink everything I do.

Then came high school. I finally quit football and soccer, finally stood up to my father, and started coming to terms with myself — finding a way to express it all: writing. It was the perfect therapy for that pain. I could finally let out emotions I'd spent years trying to bury, and turn those negative feelings into something beautiful.

Quote: Even in this rotten world, I still want to walk the path I believe in!
Quote-By: Bruno Bucciarati — JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

# Chapter 04
Label: The Craft
Title: What I Write About, and How

Body:
I love to write about romance and deep topics. None of my characters are perfect — every person has their own flaws. I like putting them through tough hardships, fundamentally changing them through those events, preferably for the better, and hopefully moving the reader too.

My stories should feel like something you can see actually happening — relatable, not complicated just to be complicated. Every story starts from something I'm fixating on deeply: a concept, a scene, or just a cool thing I saw. And for anyone paying close attention, I like to slip in subtle references to the things I'm into.

# Chapter 05
Label: Beyond the Page
Title: Other Interests

Body:
I'm Top 500 in two rhythm games, and hyper-competitive in every other game I play — I never let myself play just for fun, only to actually get better at what I enjoy.

I've played in my school's symphonic and marching band since 6th grade — tuba indoors, sousaphone on the field — five years running. I also love watching anime and reading manga: Chainsaw Man, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, Bleach, and plenty more.
